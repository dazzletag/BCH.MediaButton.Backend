# Media Button — How to Use It

Pick the section that matches your device.

---

## If your device has a **switch**

This is the simple version. A small box with one switch.

### To start

**Flip the switch ON.**

After a moment, music will start and photos will appear on the screen.

### To stop

**Flip the switch OFF.**

The music and photos stop straight away.

That's it — there's nothing else to learn.

### If it's not working

1. Check the switch is fully ON (not halfway).
2. Wait about 10 seconds — sometimes the music takes a moment to start.
3. If still nothing, **unplug the power**, wait 30 seconds, then **plug it back in**. Give it a minute to wake up, then try the switch again.
4. If that doesn't help, please tell the care home staff.

---

## If your device has a **TV remote**

This version has a small TV-style remote control with arrow buttons.

### To start

**Press any button on the remote.**

The screen will wake up and show a menu.

### Using the menu

Use the buttons on the remote like this:

- **Up / Down arrows** — move through the menu
- **OK** (or the round button in the middle) — choose the highlighted item
- **Back** — go back to the previous menu
- **Pause / Play** — pause or restart the current song or video

The menu usually offers:

- **Videos** — videos already on the device, ready to watch
- **Photos** — a slideshow of saved photos
- **Radio** — live radio stations

### To stop

**Press the Back button** until you're back at the main menu.

Or just leave it — the screen will dim on its own after a while.

### If it's not working

1. Try a different remote button — sometimes a button needs a firmer press.
2. Make sure nothing is blocking the small black sensor on the front of the box.
3. If the screen is frozen or black, **unplug the power**, wait 30 seconds, then **plug it back in**. Give it a minute to wake up.
4. If that doesn't help, please tell the care home staff.

---

## A few good things to know

- The device chooses music and videos based on what the resident enjoys. The care home updates this from time to time.
- The screen and speakers stay quiet between sessions to save power — that's normal.
- You can leave it running as long as you like.
